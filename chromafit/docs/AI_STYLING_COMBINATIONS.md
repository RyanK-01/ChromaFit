## 🎭 Style Variations

For EACH combination, the AI generates 3 different style variations:

1. **Classic Style** - Timeless design with traditional colors and cuts
2. **Modern Trendy** - Contemporary with current fashion trends and bold choices
3. **Elegant Sophisticated** - Refined details with premium aesthetics

The system randomly selects one variation per generation, so **clicking generate multiple times gives you different looks!**

## 🚀 How to Get Multiple Variations

**Method 1: Click Generate Multiple Times**
- Select your environment (e.g., "Casual")
- Select your occasion (e.g., "Party")
- Click "Generate Styled Outfit Image"
- Wait for first image
- Click again for a different variation!

**Method 2: Change Combinations**
- Try different environment + occasion pairs
- Each combination has unique outfit descriptions
- 35 total combinations to explore!
